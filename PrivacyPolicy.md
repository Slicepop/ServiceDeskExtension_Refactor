# Privacy Policy for Hephaestus

**Effective Date:** August 11, 2026

Western Michigan University Homer Stryker M.D. School of Medicine ("WMed," "we," "our," or "us") developed **Hephaestus** ("the extension") to improve the WMed Service Desk experience for authorized IT technicians. This privacy policy explains what information the extension handles, how that information is used, and where it may be transmitted.

## 1. Information We Collect and Handle

Hephaestus may handle the following categories of information:

### Authentication and Identifiers

Hephaestus uses Firebase Anonymous Authentication to provide identity for extension functionality. Firebase generates an anonymous identifier for the technician. The extension does not use this anonymous identifier to determine the technician's name, password, or other personal identity information.

### Passwords and Authentication Credentials

Hephaestus does **not** collect, store, or share users' passwords or Service Desk authentication credentials. The extension does not transmit users' Service Desk passwords to Hephaestus backend services, Firebase, or other third-party services.

Authentication to the WMed Service Desk is handled by the WMed Service Desk itself. Hephaestus may process an anonymous Firebase authentication identifier for functionality such as technician presence, but this identifier is not a password or Service Desk credential.

### Service Desk Information

The extension reads information from the WMed Service Desk website that is necessary to provide its functionality. This may include:

- Service request or ticket IDs
- Ticket subjects and other ticket information displayed in the Service Desk interface
- Information about the Service Desk page currently being viewed
- Information necessary to provide ticket monitoring and notification functionality

### Technician Activity

Hephaestus may process limited technician activity information, such as which Service Desk ticket a technician is currently viewing. This information is used to provide real-time technician presence and collaboration features.

### Locally Stored Preferences

The extension may store configuration and interface preferences locally on the technician's device, such as notification or display settings.

## 2. How We Use Information

Information handled by Hephaestus is used only to provide and improve its single purpose: enhancing the WMed Service Desk experience for authorized IT technicians.

This includes:

- Displaying which technicians are viewing a Service Desk ticket
- Providing ticket queue monitoring and notifications
- Streamlining Service Desk workflows and ticket-related actions
- Providing interface enhancements and productivity features
- Maintaining the functionality, security, and reliability of the extension

Hephaestus does not use Service Desk information for advertising, behavioral profiling, or unrelated purposes.

## 3. How Information Is Collected

Information may be collected or processed through:

- The WMed Service Desk website and its APIs
- Information generated by the extension while an authorized technician uses the Service Desk
- Firebase Anonymous Authentication
- Communication between the extension and Hephaestus backend services

Information read from the Service Desk is processed only as necessary to provide the extension's Service Desk functionality.

## 4. Sharing and Third-Party Services

Information handled by Hephaestus may be transmitted to the following services when necessary to provide the extension's functionality:

### WMed Service Desk

Hephaestus reads Service Desk information from WMed's internal Service Desk system to provide interface enhancements, ticket workflows, and monitoring functionality.

### Hephaestus Backend Services

The extension communicates with backend services operated for Hephaestus. These services process information necessary to provide features such as technician presence, ticket monitoring, authentication, and notifications.

### Firebase

Hephaestus uses Firebase services, including Firebase Anonymous Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database, to provide authentication and real-time technician presence functionality.

We do not sell user data or transfer user data to advertising networks, data brokers, or other parties for advertising or profiling.

Information is shared with external service providers only when necessary to provide, maintain, secure, or improve the extension's disclosed functionality.

## 5. Data Retention

Hephaestus does not create persistent user profiles for advertising or profiling purposes.

Information stored by Firebase or Hephaestus backend services is retained only as necessary to provide the applicable functionality, maintain the service, or protect the security and reliability of the system.

Locally stored extension preferences remain on the technician's device until they are removed by the extension, the user, or the browser.

## 6. Data Security

Hephaestus uses reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect information it handles. Data transmitted between the extension and its backend services is transmitted using encrypted connections.

Authentication information and other sensitive information are protected against unauthorized access.

## 7. Data Use Restrictions

Hephaestus uses information received through the extension only to provide or improve its single purpose and related operational functions.

Hephaestus does not:

- Sell user data
- Use user data for personalized or interest-based advertising
- Transfer user data to data brokers
- Use user data for creditworthiness or lending decisions
- Use Service Desk information for purposes unrelated to the extension's stated functionality

The use of information handled by Hephaestus complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
